Frequently asked questions.
Which models can I run with Kimchi?
Kimchi Coding stacks the strongest open source and open‑weight models.
Can I switch plans anytime?
Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade at any time, and changes apply right away.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept major credit cards and integrate with all major cloud provider marketplaces. Enterprise customers can request invoicing.
Do you have any special offers for startups?
Eligible companies can join our Startup Program. Reach out and we'll help you get set up.
How does the Open Source plan work?
You download Kimchi Coding and run it on your own machine or infrastructure. Bring your own inference - Kimchi's managed API, an OpenAI-compatible endpoint, or any LLM provider. No hosted inference, no rate limits imposed by us. Your hardware, your rules.
