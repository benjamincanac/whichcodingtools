FAQ
Frequently Asked Questions
What's included in the free Nimbalyst App?
Everything you need for personal use: the full Nimbalyst editor, your personal wiki, session manager, Claude Code and Codex integration, MCP server support, extensions, mockups, diagrams, data models, and git worktrees. No feature limits, no trial period. What is the Nimbalyst Wiki?
A wiki your agents write for you. As they work, your coding agents distill their activity into pages with your notes, open questions and decisions, so you can keep up with them and decide what comes next. You can edit any page, and pages hold markdown, mockups, diagrams and 2x2s. Your personal wiki is free. See how the Wiki works &rarr;
Can I use the wiki without the Nimbalyst app?
Yes, and it is free. Add the wiki to Claude Code, Codex, Cowork, Cursor, VS Code or any MCP agent and your agent writes it as markdown files in your repo. You read and edit it in your browser, with no app and no account. Open the same files in the Nimbalyst app whenever you want visual editing, or share them with your team on Wiki Teams. Use the wiki in your coding agent &rarr;
Do I need my own API key or subscription for AI features?
Yes. You bring your own AI access and Nimbalyst provides the workspace and tools around it. Your access can be a subscription like Claude Code or Codex, an API key from Anthropic, OpenAI, or other providers, or local models through LM Studio. Nimbalyst connects to whichever you already have. See how Claude Code and Codex access works &rarr;
What does Wiki Teams add?
Wiki Teams makes the wiki multiplayer. Your whole team works in one shared wiki in real time, every teammate's agents write into the same pages, and everyone sees the same decisions, open questions and typed databases. See Wiki Teams &rarr;
How much does Wiki Teams cost?
Wiki Teams is $20 per user per month, or $200 per user per year. It is in Beta right now and free to use while it is, so you can bring your whole team in and try it on real work before you pay anything. How do we get into the Wiki Teams Beta?
If you already have a local wiki, ask your agent to move it to Wiki Teams, or run /nimbalyst-wiki:team in Claude Code. Your agent signs you in, creates the team and moves every page. Then invite your teammates by email and everyone works in the same wiki right away. No approval step and no card required.
