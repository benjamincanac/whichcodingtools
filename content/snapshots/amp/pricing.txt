What if I use all of the included usage on a subscription?
You can add paid credits to keep using Amp until your subscription usage replenishes at the end of each monthly
period.
Can I use an Amp subscription in a team workspace?
Yes. You can start a subscription on your own, and your workspace will pay for
it. Workspace admins can also start subscriptions for their members (from the workspace members list ). There's no way yet to automatically start a new subscription for each member
of a workspace.
How much agent usage is included in the subscription plans?
We give at least the subscription cost per month in agent usage. Depending on
your usage patterns, you may get extra usage.
Are there any additional fees for using a ChatGPT subscription ?
No. Amp subscribers pay no per-token fees for usage through a linked ChatGPT subscription or other third-party subscription. You can use as many tokens as your third-party
subscription allows.
Why are the limits so generous?
We want everyone to join us living life with parallel agents running in orbs at all times. For your monthly subscription price, you get at least that much usage
of the agent with tokens from frontier models, a ridiculous amount of orb usage so
you don't need to count the minutes, and unlimited usage of your other linked AI
subscriptions.
How do I cancel my subscription?
You can cancel at any time in your user settings , and your subscription will not renew. You can keep using it for the rest of
the monthly period. If you want a refund of your current month minus any actual
usage costs you have incurred, email us .
Are there any restrictions on how I can use my subscription?
Each human can have at most two subscriptions. You can't directly resell the
resource quotas from your subscription. The orbs are intended for use with Amp
and are not to be used in violation of our Acceptable Use Policy .
Is there an educational discount?
Students and teachers can subscribe to Amp for $10/month with the Education
Discount. Declare, on your honor, that you are a student or teacher to get it.
Other questions?
For general help with Amp, post on X and mention @AmpCode , or email amp-devs@ampcode.com .
