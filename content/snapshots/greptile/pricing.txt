FAQ
How does pricing work?
Starter is free for one active developer and includes unlimited repositories and 50 credits per month. Pro is $30 per seat per month and includes 50 credits per seat. Additional credits are $1 each.
Base review: 1 credit
Plus review: 3 credits
Apex review: 10 credits
Can I prepay for a discount?
We offer custom pricing for annual and multi-year contracts. Let's chat
.
How do I cancel my subscription?
You can cancel your subscription at any time through your account settings. If you have any questions, please contact us.
