Questions & Answers
What is the right plan for me?
If you're just getting started, try the Free Trial to experience Sweep's capabilities. For regular use, Basic ($10/mo) offers unlimited autocomplete. Pro ($20/mo) is ideal for power users who need more API credits and priority support. Ultra ($60/mo) is for developers who want maximum productivity with the highest API credit allocation.
How do API credits work?
API credits are consumed when you use AI features like chat, code generation, and advanced completions. Autocomplete is unlimited on paid plans. Each plan includes a monthly credit allocation, and you can monitor your usage in the Balance tab of your account. You can configure top-up settings to automatically replenish your credits.
Do you train on my code?
Sweep offers Privacy Mode which ensures your code is never trained on. When Privacy Mode is enabled, your code is only used to provide AI suggestions and is never stored or used for model training. You can enable this in your account settings.
Which JetBrains IDEs are supported?
Sweep works with all JetBrains IDEs including IntelliJ IDEA, PyCharm, WebStorm, GoLand, PhpStorm, Rider, CLion, RubyMine, and more. Simply install the plugin from the JetBrains Marketplace.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes, you can cancel your subscription at any time from your account settings. You'll continue to have access to your plan features until the end of your billing period. There are no long-term commitments or cancellation fees.
Do you offer discounts for students or open source contributors?
Yes! We offer special pricing for students, educators, and open source maintainers. Contact us at team@sweep.dev with verification of your status to learn more about our discount programs.
