Why hyper?
Cost Efficiency Hyper offers LLM inference that's more cost effective than direct provider costs.
Specialized for Coding It is specifically designed to streamline agentic AI coding workflows for the Crush developer community, allowing users to complete full workflows that were previously too expensive.
An Emphasis on Quality and Reliability Hyper's designed to provide consistent, high-quality AI outputs across its supported coding models.
Granular Control Hyper's a pro tool. It offers fine-grained API key management (master and sub keys), giving you and your team better governance over their budgets and usage.
What makes it good?
Infrastructure Mastery Unlike a standard proxy or router that simply passes traffic, Hyper is an optimized LLM provider that manages the underlying infrastructure, including orchestration, scheduling, and inference engines.
Strategic Partnerships Hyper leverages deep partnerships with AI cloud providers and silicon providers to optimize costs at the hardware and kernel level, rather than just focusing on routing margins.
Intelligent Routing Beyond simple proxying, the Hyper roadmap includes intelligent model selection, which automatically delegates tasks to the most cost-effective or specialized model for specific steps in coding workflows.
Hypercredits
Hypercredits are the core of Hyper's usage-based pricing model, where credits map directly to token costs. 1 Hypercredit is currently 5¢, and they're distributed based on your subscription tier.
We believe a prepaid system with credits is easier and more transparent for users to consume versus swiping a credit card and being surprised with a bill later. This is valid both when buying one-off credit bundles or purchasing a subscriptions.
