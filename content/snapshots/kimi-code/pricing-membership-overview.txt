What happens to legacy plans?
Andante, Moderato, Allegretto, and Allegro are legacy plans. Existing subscribers keep their plan names, benefit rules, and auto-renewal arrangements. They can continue using and renewing their plan, or upgrade through the options shown on the subscription page. Switching to a new plan is optional.
Legacy plans retain both a rolling 5-hour Kimi Code limit and a weekly limit. New plans with Kimi Code access retain the rolling 5-hour limit and have no weekly limit. Both use the monthly shared credit pool. Go does not include Kimi Code; Plus and higher new plans do. Legacy Andante and higher plans retain Kimi Code access.
See the official Kimi Code membership guide for details.
Billing
Credit pool : All membership features, including website deployment, Deep Research, PPT, Kimi Code, Kimi Work, Kimi Claw, K3, and K3 Swarm, share one credit pool and are deducted based on actual token consumption. If any feature uses up the credits, your use of other features will also be affected.
Kimi Code limits : New plans with Kimi Code access (Plus and higher) have a rolling 5-hour usage limit and no weekly limit. Legacy plans retain both rolling 5-hour and weekly limits. These are usage limits, not a cap of five hours of use per week, and apply only to Kimi Code. Exhausting the monthly shared credit pool also affects Kimi Code.
Monthly refresh : Credits are refreshed automatically each billing cycle.
What is a token?
A token is the smallest unit of text processed by an LLM. In Chinese, 1 Chinese character is usually about 1–2 tokens; in English, 1 word is usually about 1–1.5 tokens. Both what you enter and what Kimi outputs consume tokens.
Complex tasks and longer context generally consume more tokens. Actual usage depends on the model, tools, and plan; check your usage details. You can check your remaining credits anytime on the Settings → Subscription page.
Annual Subscription Discount
Compared with 12 months at the monthly price, annual billing for Ultra saves ¥3,360/year (¥1,399 × 12 − ¥13,428), the largest annual saving among the current plans.
FAQs
How do I request an invoice?
Visit kimi.com , click your avatar in the lower-left corner → Settings → Subscription → Billing → Request Invoice.
Change the recipient email : Go to the subscription page, click the “Invoiced” button for the corresponding order, update the email address, and click “Resend”.
Incorrect invoice information : Please email [email protected] with the original invoice and the correct invoice information. We will reissue it for you as soon as possible.
How do I upgrade my membership plan?
Visit kimi.com , click your avatar in the lower-left corner → Membership Plan, select the new plan you want, and complete payment.
When you upgrade, we will calculate a refund based on the remaining days of your current membership. The new membership benefits take effect immediately on the upgrade date, with no waiting required.
How long are membership benefits valid?
Both monthly and annual subscriptions receive credits on a monthly subscription cycle. Check the subscription page for your exact refresh time. Unused credits do not roll over.
Unused benefits during the subscription period will be automatically cleared after expiry. Please use them in time.
How do I check my credit usage?
Web : Click your avatar in the lower-left corner → Settings → Subscription
App : My → Membership Plan → Subscription
You can view your current credit balance as a percentage, the next refresh time, and the latest 1,000 usage records, including usage time, feature, and consumption percentage.
Usage detail data may have a short delay. Please refer to the current credit display.
What happens if I run out of credits?
Tasks already in progress can be completed normally. New tasks will show an insufficient credits prompt. You can:
Wait for credits to refresh automatically each month; for a Kimi Code usage limit, check the console for the reset time (weekly limits apply only to legacy plans)
Upgrade to a higher-tier membership for more credits
Purchase an Extra Usage pack : once enabled, you pay as you go and don't need to wait for credits to refresh
Join official campaigns to receive bonus credits
Can I use all credits on one feature?
Yes. All membership features share one credit pool, and you can allocate it freely. Please note: if one feature uses up your credits, other features will also be affected. New plans with Kimi Code access (Plus and higher) have a rolling 5-hour usage limit and no weekly limit. Legacy plans retain both rolling 5-hour and weekly limits. These are usage limits, not a cap of five hours of use per week, and apply only to Kimi Code. Exhausting the monthly shared credit pool also affects Kimi Code.
Are paid services refundable?
Paid services are virtual services. Once successfully purchased and activated, they are deemed consumed. Unless otherwise provided by laws and regulations, they cannot be refunded or transferred. Please carefully check the service and benefit details, price, and validity period before purchase.
Reminder on purchases by minors
Minors under the age of 18 should read the relevant agreements under the guidance of their guardians and may purchase paid services only with guardian consent. Guardians should properly safeguard accounts, devices, and passwords to prevent minors from making paid purchases without consent.
