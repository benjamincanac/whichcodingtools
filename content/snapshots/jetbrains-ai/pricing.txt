JetBrains AI plans and usage
AI Assistant is a collection of specific features that become available to users through access to the JetBrains AI service . This service connects you, as a product user, to various large language models ( LLMs ) and enables AI-powered features in JetBrains products.
To access the JetBrains AI service, you usually need a JetBrains AI subscription. To start a free trial or activate a subscription, refer to Use JetBrains AI subscription . This article describes the available license tiers, their price, and usage volume for individual and organizational users.
AI Assistant provides other ways to activate AI-powered features . However, using the JetBrains AI service subscription ensures that all features are available and work as expected.
In organizations that have been migrated to JetBrains Central , you do not need a separate JetBrains AI subscription. Instead, an administrator grants you AI access, and your usage draws from a pool of resources shared across the whole organization. For details, refer to the Organizations (JetBrains Central) tab in Available license tiers .
Due to the restrictions imposed by providers of the AI models connected to JetBrains AI, such as OpenAI, Google, and Anthropic, usage of the JetBrains AI service is restricted to the allowed territories .
Since July 13, 2026, the JetBrains AI service is no longer available in China. You can still use AI Assistant there with your own API keys or third-party and local models . In JetBrains IDEs 2025.3.2 and later, you can also install ACP-compatible agents from the ACP registry . Using ACP agents does not require a JetBrains AI subscription.
Available license tiers
JetBrains AI offers several license tiers – AI Free [1] , AI Pro , AI Ultimate [1] , and AI Enterprise :
The AI Free , AI Pro , and AI Ultimate license tiers are available for both individual users and organizations.
The AI Enterprise license tier is available only to organizations as a part of JetBrains IDE services .
There is also an AI Trial license tier intended for evaluation and available to both individual and organizational users.
[1] The AI Free and AI Ultimate tiers are available in most JetBrains IDEs starting from version 2025.1 , though some restrictions may apply.
Each license provides a quota – a resource that regulates access to AI-powered features. It is represented by AI credits , which are consumed when you use AI features that rely on cloud models.

Organizations are being migrated to JetBrains Central gradually. In an organization that uses the new model, AI access is managed separately from product licenses: an admin can grant or revoke AI access and set how many AI credits each user can spend per month by configuring a default limit for users and, if needed, individual per-user limits.
AI credits transferred from the organization's existing licenses that included AI resources are placed in a single shared pool rather than assigned to individual users. Recurring credits provided through eligible licenses are added to this pool monthly, and purchased AI credits are added to the same pool. Purchased AI credits expire 12 months after purchase, while AI credits transferred from a license expire on the last day of the calendar month in which the original license expires. This differs from the previous per-user model, in which each user's monthly allocation of AI credits expired when it renewed.
During migration, each eligible license that included AI resources contributes a credit allocation based on its former monthly AI credit allocation. In addition, these license tiers have other limitations:
The AI Free license tier is not available in Android Studio.
The AI Free license tier is not available in Community Editions of PyCharm and IntelliJ IDEA, as well as in PyCharm Unified when used for free.
The AI Free license tier is not automatically included in any IDE trial license.
The AI Pro license tier provided for free with the All Products Pack or dotUltimate subscription, as well as the AI Free license tier, cannot be activated if the IDE license is obtained through the License Server .
The AI Pro license tier is provided for free with DataSpell .
The AI Free and AI Ultimate license tiers are only available in the serviceable territories .
Manage a license
This section provides instructions on how to manage a JetBrains AI license.
Manage multiple licenses
If you have multiple JetBrains AI licenses, you can manage them in the AI Chat tool window.
In the AI Chat tool window, click the Options button.
Select the Manage License option from the list.
This opens a License pane where you can review the license details:
If you have multiple licenses, you can choose the one you want to use by selecting it from the list and clicking Activate .
To refresh the list of licenses, click Refresh license list .
Log out of JetBrains AI
If you need to log out of JetBrains AI, you can do so in the settings.
Navigate to Settings | Tools | AI Assistant | Providers & API keys .
In the JetBrains AI section, click Log out .
After that, you can configure other authentication options to keep using AI Assistant.
If you want to log in again using your JetBrains AI service subscription, refer to the Enable JetBrains AI alongside custom models section.
Add and manage AI credits
You can purchase additional AI credits to continue using AI features after the quota included in your license tier is exhausted. Once purchased, these credits are automatically added to your balance and consumed the same way as your regular quota. You can purchase and manage them through the JetBrains AI widget in your IDE or in JetBrains Account settings.
Using the JetBrains AI widget
The JetBrains AI widget located in the window header toolbar lets you purchase additional AI credits and track your usage.
Purchasing AI credits through the widget is available only for personal accounts.
Add AI credits
Click Buy near the credits counter.
On the eStore Order Checkout page, specify the number of AI credits you want to purchase.
Provide any other required information and submit your order.
Track AI credits usage
The top-up credits counter shows how many purchased AI credits you currently have, with an option to add more, if needed.
Using JetBrains Account
You can purchase AI credits and view usage information in your JetBrains Account settings.
If your organization has been migrated to the shared credit pool model , manage AI credits in JetBrains Central Console instead.
Add AI credits
Log in to your JetBrains Account .
Navigate to the JetBrains AI Settings section.
In the Top-up AI Credits for personal use card, click the Add AI Credits button.
AI credits can be purchased only if you already have an active JetBrains AI license.
Pass eStore Identification and proceed to checkout.
On the eStore Order Checkout page, specify the number of AI credits you want to purchase.
Provide any other required information and submit your order.
Track AI credits usage
Log in to your JetBrains Account .
Navigate to the JetBrains AI Settings section.
Review the cards. The upper card displays your personal AI credits, while the lower one displays AI credits assigned to you by your organization.
Currently, the progress bar has only two states:
Fully filled as long as there is at least one AI credit.
Empty when the balance reaches zero.
This implementation is temporary and will be improved in the future.

Using JetBrains Central Console
Organizations that have been migrated to the shared credit pool model manage AI credits in JetBrains Central Console .
For instructions, refer to the JetBrains Central Console documentation:
Manage AI access – grant or revoke AI access to users.
Manage AI credits – add AI credits, set limits, and configure an auto-purchase schedule for your organization.
Monitor AI credits usage – track how AI credits are consumed across your organization.
Review AI credits usage report
At the beginning of each month, you receive a report with details of your AI credits usage for the previous month. The report is sent to the email address associated with your JetBrains Account .
The report provides various details, but the following fields are the most important for interpreting your AI credits usage:
Period – the date range covered by the report.
Total – the total number of AI credits spent during the specified period. Only purchased AI credits are counted.
Including credits for free – the number of AI credits spent that were obtained at no cost through discounts or promotional offers.
Quota and AI credits FAQ
This section provides answers to common questions about quota and AI credits. What is quota and what are AI credits?
Quota is a monthly resource measured in AI credits, which are consumed when you use AI-powered features.
The number of AI credits available to you matches the price of your subscription. Each AI credit corresponds to $1 USD charged in your local currency. How much usage do I get from one AI credit?
One AI credit gives you about:
~10 requests to generate code in AI Chat.
~40 requests to generate code in the editor.
~25 requests to explain selected code fragments with AI.
~280 requests to write documentation.
~140 requests to generate commit messages.
This is a rough approximation, as the final cost of AI actions depends on multiple factors . When does my quota renew?
The quota reset occurs every 30 days from the date when you first used your JetBrains AI license. How is quota usage calculated?
Quota usage is calculated dynamically for each interaction based on several factors, primarily the number of tokens used — units of text from both your input and the AI’s response. Since token usage can vary depending on the length, complexity, and context of a request, the quota cost may differ from one interaction to another. How can I track my quota usage?
Quota usage can be tracked in the JetBrains AI widget located in the toolbar in the window header:
The monthly credits left counter shows how many AI credits you have left out of your subscription quota . The progress bar reflects overall usage and decreases as you use cloud-based features. The date under the progress bar indicates when the monthly quota resets. What to do if the quota usage limit is reached?
Once the quota for the current period is exhausted, you have the following options:
Wait for the next 30-day cycle when the quota resets.
(Trial, AI Pro, AI Ultimate) Purchase AI credits to continue using AI features beyond your monthly quota.
Use a different license if you have multiple JetBrains AI licenses .
Upgrade to a higher tier to renew the quota. This also increases the total number of AI credits available per 30-day period.
Configure AI Assistant to use models from third-party providers or set up agents. What can lead to high quota usage?
Several factors can increase quota consumption:
Long chat threads: Using a single chat instance for multiple topics causes context to grow exponentially, increasing token consumption and quickly using up your quota.
Expensive models: Models with higher token costs ( marked with the icon) consume quota faster.
Use of agents: Extensive use of agents leads to higher quota consumption.
How to reduce quota usage:
Start a new chat for each unrelated topic or task.
Switch to more cost-effective models.
For heavy agentic workloads, consider upgrading to the AI Ultimate license tier, which has a larger monthly quota. Can I buy more AI credits?
Yes, you can purchase additional AI credits to continue using AI features after the dedicated quota included in your license tier is exhausted.
You can add AI credits to your balance in the settings of your JetBrains Account . How are purchased AI credits spent?
AI Assistant starts using your purchased AI credits only after your monthly quota is fully used. They remain in use until the monthly quota is renewed. Once the new monthly quota becomes available, AI Assistant switches back to using it. Any remaining purchased AI credits stay in your balance and will be used again only after the next monthly quota runs out. Where can I track the usage of purchased AI credits?
Purchased AI credits can be tracked in the JetBrains AI widget located in the toolbar in the window header:
As an alternative, you can also track purchased AI credits in the settings of your JetBrains Account .
In addition, a report with details of your purchased AI credits usage for the previous month is sent at the beginning of each month. Do purchased AI credits expire?
Purchased AI credits stay valid for 12 months from the date they were added, unless specified or agreed differently. They remain available to you until they are used or expire. Can I use purchased AI credits in other licenses?
Purchased AI credits can be used across all JetBrains AI licenses purchased under the same customer account. This means that any AI credits you purchased yourself can be used only for licenses you personally bought.
Likewise, AI credits provided by your organization can be used only for licenses assigned to you by the organization. How are purchased AI credits used in an organization?
Purchased AI credits are shared among all JetBrains AI licenses purchased by your organization. Administrators can set the limit on how many AI credits can be used per subscription in the settings of your organization account. Are there any regional limitations on using purchased AI credits?
Purchased AI credits are not available for use in China.
