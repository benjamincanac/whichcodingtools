Frequently Asked Questions
### How do I get and use a Pro trial?
New subscribers who register for TRAE on or after February 1, 2026 are eligible for a one-time, 7-day free Pro trial.
A new subscriber refers to a user who is purchasing any paid plan for the first time. Eligibility for the trial, introductory pricing, or promotional offers may be determined based on factors such as device usage history, payment behavior, or refund history.
Each user is eligible for the Pro trial only once. If multiple accounts are used to obtain additional trials, TRAE reserves the right to revoke account access.
After the trial period ends, your subscription will automatically renew as a Pro plan unless canceled before the trial expires.
### What happens when I run out of usage?
When your included usage is exhausted, you'll need to upgrade to a higher plan to get more usage. Paid users can also enable On-Demand Usage to continue using paid models.
On-Demand Usage allows you to keep using paid models after your monthly balance is exhausted. Additional usage is billed based on actual token consumption.
