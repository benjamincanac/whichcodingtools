// faq
Questions, answered.
Still curious? Read the pricing & limits docs . Or email support@commandcode.ai .
Which plan is right for you?
Go ($1) starts you off — open-source models, big budgets in tiny dollars. GOAT ($10) is for developers who live on open models — $70 of usage on 29+ models. Pro ($20) adds premium models to the shared pool — up to $80 of usage on per-model allowances. Max 10× ($100) keeps up with daily shipping. Max 20× ($200) goes all-out — every model, no caps. Teams and Enterprise pool credits for the whole crew.
How does pricing work?
Flat monthly subscriptions for developers with 2-10x better than API prices. A bucket of model credits. Buy on demand extra credits anytime, you spend at the underlying API rate — zero markup. Run out? Top up, or flip on auto-reload. Top-up credits roll over and never expire.
Out of credits?
Top up from Studio > Billing — or turn on auto-reload. Extras credits are on-demand and optional auto-reloadable, buy at model cost, roll over, and never expire.
Can I change my mind?
Always. Upgrade, downgrade, or cancel from Studio > Billing . No hoops, no penalties, no hard feelings. You keep access until the end of your billing cycle, and any unused credits roll over forever.
Where's my usage?
Right here: Usage . Per-request cost, token counts, the models, the whole timeline.
Do you train on my code?
No. Never. Taste runs on your codebase and stores its learning data on your machine — and only your machine. See the Privacy Policy .
How does team billing work?
Flat plan. Pooled credits. Every member of the team draws from one shared bucket. Org admins manage seats, billing, and usage from org settings.
Which payment methods?
All major credit and debit cards via Studio > Billing . Need invoice or wire? Email support@commandcode.ai about Enterprise.
Where are models hosted?
Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, and Azure handle commercial and some open source models on US infrastructure (EU on demand). Open-source routes globally — US, EU, Singapore. See the Privacy Policy .
Resellers?
Nope. Subscriptions sell directly through commandcode.ai only.
And open-source privacy?
Go ($1) is built for international developers — the best coding agent, for everyone. Open-source models route through US, EU, and Singapore for reliable global access. Need more? Talk to us about Team & Enterprise .
More questions?
Join the Discord . Or email support@commandcode.ai for anything private.
