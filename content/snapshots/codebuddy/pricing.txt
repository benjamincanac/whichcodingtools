During the Free plan's promotional period, all models are available, code completion is unlimited, and up to 99 automated tasks are included. Standard quotas resume when the promotion ends.
After subscribing to Pro or starting the 7-day Pro trial, the Free plan's 100 credits are no longer issued separately.
The promotional bonus is a limited-time offer. Users who make a chat request that day are considered active. See the Limited-Time Daily Activity Reward Terms for details.
Base and bonus credits are issued monthly and are valid for that month; they do not roll over. Bonus credits are valid for one month from the date they are credited.
