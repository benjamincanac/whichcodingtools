Questions
Is Jcode free?
Yes. Jcode is free and open source under the MIT license, with no feature gates. Bring your own API keys, use supported OAuth subscriptions, or run local models.
What am I agreeing to when I subscribe?
You pick a monthly plan from $10 to $1,000, and Stripe charges that amount every month while you are subscribed. Every successful payment grants inference credit worth double the plan price, so a $10 plan gets $20 of credit and a $100 plan gets $200. The credit is applied first, then inference moves to usage-based billing at provider API prices in addition to the subscription.
What happens after my credit runs out?
You keep working at provider API prices. A monthly usage limit is set automatically to 10x your plan price, so a $10 plan can use up to $100 beyond its credit and a $100 plan up to $1,000. New hosted-model usage pauses at the limit, and you can adjust it in your account.
What is Jcode Cloud?
Every plan includes Jcode Cloud, a private hosted machine that runs your Jcode sessions. You get 50 machine hours each month for every $10 of plan, so a $100 plan gets 500 hours, and plans from $150 up can keep the machine running all month. The machine sleeps after 30 minutes without a connected client, so idle time does not count.
Can I change plans?
Yes. Sign in, pick a larger or smaller plan on this page, and choose Switch. The new plan is billed, with its double credit, from your next monthly payment, and your usage limit moves to 10x the new plan right away.
Can I keep using Jcode without hosted models?
Yes. You can keep using your own provider credentials, supported OAuth access, or local models without activating hosted inference.
Do you offer plans for organizations?
Yes. Enterprise plans can include centralized billing and usage management, deployment and integration guidance, security and procurement review, and priority support. Contact us about enterprise to discuss your requirements.
