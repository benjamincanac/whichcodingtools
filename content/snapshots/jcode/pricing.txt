Questions
Is Jcode free?
Yes. Jcode is free and open source under the MIT license, with no feature gates. Bring your own API keys, use supported OAuth subscriptions, or run local models.
What am I agreeing to when I subscribe?
Stripe charges $10 every month while you are subscribed. Every successful monthly payment grants $20 of inference credit, so your first $20 of usage each month costs you $10. The credit is applied first, then inference moves to usage-based billing at provider API prices in addition to the subscription.
How does the monthly hard cap work?
The cap limits usage billed after your $20 credit, so the most you can be charged in a month is $10 plus your cap. Pick $10, $20, $50, $100, $200, or $1,000 when you subscribe (the default is $100), then set any amount in your account, from $0 with no product-defined maximum. New hosted-model usage stops at the cap, and changing it does not erase usage already incurred. Warning emails start at $20 of usage and double to $40, $80, and so on as usage grows.
Can I keep using Jcode without hosted models?
Yes. You can keep using your own provider credentials, supported OAuth access, or local models without activating hosted inference.
Do you offer plans for organizations?
Yes. Enterprise plans can include centralized billing and usage management, deployment and integration guidance, security and procurement review, and priority support. Contact us about enterprise to discuss your requirements.
