Questions
Is Jcode free?
Yes. Jcode is free and open source under the MIT license, with no feature gates. Bring your own API keys, use supported OAuth subscriptions, or run local models.
What am I agreeing to when I subscribe?
Stripe charges $10 every month while you are subscribed. Every successful monthly payment grants $20 of inference credit, making the first $20 of usage effectively 50% off provider API pricing. The credit is applied first, then inference moves to usage-based billing at 10% off provider API prices (90% of the API price) in addition to the subscription.
How does the monthly hard cap work?
It defaults to $100 per UTC month. You can lower it to $0 or raise it without a product-defined maximum. New hosted-model usage stops at the cap, and changing it does not erase usage already incurred. Warning emails start at $20 of usage and double to $40, $80, and later thresholds at or below the cap.
Can I keep using Jcode without hosted models?
Yes. You can keep using your own provider credentials, supported OAuth access, or local models without activating hosted inference.
Do you offer plans for organizations?
Yes. Enterprise plans can include centralized billing and usage management, deployment and integration guidance, security and procurement review, and priority support. Contact enterprise sales to discuss your requirements.
