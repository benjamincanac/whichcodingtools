Billing details
Subscription Recurring $10 charge each month while subscribed.
Monthly credit Every successful $10 payment grants $20 of inference credit, double what you pay. Fresh credit arrives each month and does not accumulate.
Jcode Cloud Subscribing includes Jcode Cloud with 50 machine hours each month, in proportion to your plan (50 hours per $10).
Remote Rust builds Not included on the $10 plan. Remote Rust builds start at $20 (5,000 a month, 2,500 per $10).
After credit Raise your extra usage limit to continue at provider API prices once the credit is used, billed by usage in addition to the subscription.
Extra usage limit Defaults to $0 of additional inference per UTC month after your credit. Raise it any time in your account. Your custom limit is kept when you change plans.
