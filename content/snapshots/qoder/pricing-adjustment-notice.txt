Pricing Update: End of Discount & Teams Plan Changes
Copy page
Copy page
Thank you for your trust and support of Qoder. Over the past year, we have explored more possibilities in AI-powered programming together and heard a lot of feedback on what you'd like to see. To deliver a better product experience, we will be making changes to our subscription plans starting April 30, 2026 at 10:00 (Singapore Time) . The key adjustments are as follows:
End of Individual Plan Half-Price Promotion : The 50% discount on individual subscription plans (Pro, Pro+ and Ultra) and personal add-on credits will end, reverting to standard pricing.
Teams Plan Specification and Price Adjustment : The per-seat monthly quota will increase from 2,000 Credits to 3,000 Credits, while the seat price will be adjusted from $30 to $40.
Details are provided below.
​
Individual Plan: End of Limited-Time Half-Price Promotion
Since September 2025, Qoder has offered a limited-time 50% discount on Pro, Pro+ and Ultra subscriptions, as well as personal add-on credits (see Promotion Page
). This promotion will officially end on April 30, 2026 at 10:00 (Singapore Time) . After the promotion ends, both new purchases and renewals will revert to standard pricing:
Promotional Price (Current) Standard Price (After Promotion) Included Credits
Pro $10/month $20/month 2,000 Credits
Pro+ $30/month $60/month 6,000 Credits
Ultra $100/month $200/month 20,000 Credits
Personal Add-on Credits $10 $20 1,000 Credits
​
Teams Plan: Specification and Price Adjustment
Starting April 30, 2026 at 10:00 (Singapore Time), the Teams Plan will undergo the following specification and price adjustments. Shared add-on credits pricing remains unchanged:
Before Adjustment After Adjustment
Seat Price $30/seat/month $40/seat/month
Included Credits 2,000 Credits/seat/month 3,000 Credits/seat/month
The Teams Plan will progressively introduce new capabilities for team collaboration and management, including but not limited to:
BYOK : Support for organizations to configure their own model APIs on demand.
Security Controls : Support for security controls over MCP and Skills, enabling unified management of available tools and capability boundaries for team members.
Plugin Management and Distribution : Unified management and distribution of Plugins including Rules, MCP, Skills, and Hooks, providing teams with standardized work environments.
Knowledge : A self-iterating enterprise knowledge capability based on team RepoWiki and programming experiences from conversations. It continuously updates as the team works, enabling the Agent to deeply understand team context.
These features are designed to help teams benefit from AI-powered programming acceleration while ensuring tool compliance and collaboration consistency.
​
FAQ
Q: I'm an existing individual plan subscriber. Will the new pricing take effect immediately after the promotion ends?
When the half-price promotion ends, your current paid billing cycle will not be affected. The new standard pricing will take effect on your next renewal or new purchase. If you have auto-renewal enabled, the renewal will be charged at the new standard price.
Q: When will the new Teams subscription pricing and quotas take effect?
The new subscription prices will take effect for Teams new purchases, upgrades, and renewals from April 30, 2026, 10:00 (Singapore Time), and the per-seat resource quota will be updated to 3,000 Credits/seat accordingly. For existing organizations, the quotas of seats that have already been consumed will remain unchanged.
Q: I'm an individual plan paid user. Can I upgrade to Teams?
Yes, you can upgrade at any time and invite team members to join.
Q: Can I get a refund?
For orders purchased on the official website, if no Credits have been used within 24 hours of subscribing, you can send an email to refund@qoder.com to request a refund.
For orders purchased through third-party channels, if the redemption code has not been redeemed, you can apply for a refund through the original purchase channel. If the redemption code has already been redeemed, refunds are not supported.
If you have any other questions, please contact us at contact@qoder.com .
