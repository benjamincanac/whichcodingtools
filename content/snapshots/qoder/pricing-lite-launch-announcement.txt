# Introducing Qoder Lite: Start creating with Qoder for $10 a month
Qoder Lite is here. Starting today, users worldwide can get 1,000 Credits and a curated selection of cost-effective models for \$10 a month.
We believe everyone should be able to bring their ideas to life with Qoder. Getting started should be easy.
Lite offers a more affordable way to get started than Pro, with Qoder’s full agent capabilities ready for your real-world projects. You get more room to build: more agent requests, more models to choose from, and access to every Qoder feature, including Repo Wiki and Cloud Agents.
As your projects grow, you can upgrade at any time for more Credits and access to the full model lineup.
## A smaller plan with all the features
Lite differs from Pro, Pro+, and Ultra primarily in its model selection and monthly Credits. It includes the full set of features across the Qoder product family:
- **1,000 Credits per month**: Use them for conversations, agent tasks, and Repo Wiki. Get 1,000 bonus Credits on your first purchase and 500 bonus Credits with each renewal. This is a limited-time offer; see the [pricing page](https://qoder.com/pricing) for the latest details.
- **Cost-effective models for a wide range of tasks**: From everyday coding to complex engineering, long-context work, and multimodal tasks. See the full list below.
- **Full Qoder agent capabilities**: Use Cloud Agents, local and cloud automation, Repo Wiki, and every other Qoder feature. Each available model retains its full context window.
- **One subscription across the Qoder product family**: Qoder, Qoder IDE, the JetBrains plugin, Qoder CLI, QoderWake, Qoder Mobile, and the web app share the same Credits.
- **Customize your workflow**: Extend Qoder with Skills, Hooks, MCP, and BYOK.
## Curated models to make every Credit go further
You don’t need a flagship model for every task.
Lite pairs the fast, credit-efficient Flash series with flagship models for complex engineering and multi-step tasks. Choose the right model for the job and make your 1,000 Credits go further.
Make Qoder part of your everyday development workflow, and turn to a more powerful model when a harder problem comes along. Keep building throughout the month.
### Models available with Lite
- **Smart model tiers**: Auto and Efficient (free for paid users for a limited time).
- **Flash series**: Qwen3.8-Flash (currently free for a limited time), DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017 (currently free for a limited time), and GLM-5.3-Flash.

Credit consumption varies by model. Refer to the model selector for details.
## Choosing a plan
- **Free**: Try Qoder for free, including a 2-week Pro trial with 300 Credits in total.
- **Lite**: \$10 per month, 1,000 Credits, and a curated selection of cost-effective models for everyday use.
- **Pro**: \$20 per month, 2,000 Credits, and access to all models, including advanced tiers such as Ultimate.
When you need more usage or access to all models, you can upgrade to Pro, Pro+, or Ultra at any time.
## Limited-time launch offers
- **Double Credits on your first purchase**: Subscribe to Lite for the first time and receive 1,000 bonus Credits, for a total of 2,000 Credits in your first month.
- **Renewal bonus**: Receive 500 bonus Credits every time you renew Lite.
- **Upgrade bonus**: Upgrade from Lite to Pro and receive 500 bonus Credits.
These offers are available for a limited time. See the [pricing page](https://qoder.com/pricing) for offer details and expiration dates.
## How to subscribe
- **Already have a Qoder Global account?** Go to the [pricing page](https://qoder.com/pricing) to subscribe to Lite directly.
- **New to Qoder?** Sign up at [qoder.com](https://qoder.com) and choose Lite.
## Frequently asked questions
**What are the benefits of Qoder Lite?**
Lite gives you more room to build than Free, with 1,000 Credits per month and a curated selection of cost-effective models. You get full access to Qoder’s agent capabilities, Repo Wiki, Cloud Agents, and every other feature.
**Is Qoder Lite secure?**
Yes. Lite follows the same security and privacy standards as all Qoder products, including support for Privacy Mode.
**What happens when I run out of Credits?**
Models and tasks that require Credits will be paused. You can upgrade to Pro, purchase a Credit Pack, or wait for your Credits to refresh at the start of your next billing cycle.
**Do unused Credits roll over to the next month?**
No. Plan Credits expire at the end of the current billing cycle.
**What happens when I upgrade from Lite to Pro?**
Your Pro subscription starts as soon as your payment is complete, and your Lite subscription ends. You receive a refund based on the value of your unused Credits.
