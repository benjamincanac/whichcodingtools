FAQ
Is the terminal really free?
Yes. The macOS app is open source and free forever. Local agents run with your own API keys at no cost to you. Cloud VMs are part of the paid plans.
What are Cloud VMs?
Persistent Linux machines in the cloud: pets, not cattle. Following the Zen of cmux, a machine is a primitive, not a managed solution: it keeps its files, installed tools, and state, and what you build on it is yours. Agents clone repositories, run commands, and open pull requests there without touching your Mac. Pro includes up to 5 Cloud VMs sharing 20 vCPUs and 40 GB RAM. Team includes the same allowance for each paid seat, in one pool shared across the team.
What does Max add?
Max is $200/mo, billed monthly. It includes everything in Pro and up to 5 Cloud VMs sharing 80 vCPUs and 160 GB RAM. Pro and Team include up to 5 Cloud VMs sharing 20 vCPUs and 40 GB RAM. Your VMs draw from one pool. Run one large VM or five small ones. Paused VMs do not use the pool. Free accounts do not include Cloud VMs.
Do I still need Tailscale for the iOS app?
No. The iOS app pairs with your Mac over the built-in cmux iroh network. You can also bring your own networking, like Tailscale. Enterprise can self-host.
How does billing work?
Pro is $50/mo, or $480/yr ($40/mo) when billed yearly. Max is $200/mo and Team is $60/user/mo, both billed monthly. The terminal and local agents stay free.
Can I self-host the cloud?
Yes, on Enterprise. Run agent execution on your own infrastructure or fully air-gapped, so source code never leaves your network.
Do you have a team plan?
Yes. Team is $60/user/mo with centralized billing for your whole team and priority support.
