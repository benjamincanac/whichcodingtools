FAQ
Questions before you sign up?
What is ClinePass? ClinePass is a low-cost subscription that includes open weight models in Cline. It is designed for developers who want simple setup, generous quotas, and reliable access without managing separate provider keys.
What models does ClinePass include? The lineup includes models from Z.ai, Moonshot AI, DeepSeek, MiniMax, MiMo, and Qwen, including GLM 5.2, Kimi K3, Kimi K2.7 Code, Kimi K2.6, DeepSeek V4 Pro, DeepSeek V4 Flash, MiniMax M3, MiMo V2.5 Pro, MiMo V2.5, Qwen3.7-Max, and Qwen3.7-Plus.
What is the difference between ClinePass, Cline providers and BYOK? Cline remains open source, and bring-your-own-key workflows still work. ClinePass is the paid plan for curated included models, higher quotas, and simpler provider setup inside Cline. Cline provider gives access to a wider list of models, with pay-as-you-go API rate.
How much does ClinePass cost? For a limited time, the first month is $4.99. After the promotion period, the standard rate is $9.99/month. Additional processing fees may apply.
Can I cancel? Yes. You can cancel through your account settings before the next renewal. You keep access through the end of the current subscription term.
Can I use ClinePass with other coding agents? Yes, you can use ClinePass with other coding agents by using your ClinePass API key. Follow the setup instructions in your preferred agent.
