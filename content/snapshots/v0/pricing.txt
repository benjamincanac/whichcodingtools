Frequently Asked Questions
01 What does a "token" mean on v0?
Input tokens are measured based on what you send and output tokens are measured based on what v0 returns. Every time you send a prompt or receive output from v0, it is broken into tokens and measured. Pricing is based on how many of each are processed.
