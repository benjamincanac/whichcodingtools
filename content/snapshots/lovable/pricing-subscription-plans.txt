Subscription plans
Copy page Copy page
Compare Lovable plans, subscription tiers, billing cycles, and learn how to upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your plan.
Copy page Copy page
Lovable has a Free plan and several paid plans for individuals, teams, and organizations. For the latest plan comparison and pricing details, always refer to Lovable pricing .
Manage or cancel your subscription
Open Settings → Plans & credit usage to upgrade, downgrade, switch billing cycle, update your payment method, view invoices, or cancel. See Manage your subscription below for step-by-step instructions.
For details about how credits work, what drives usage, top-ups, and credit usage tracking, see Credits and usage .
Need help choosing or managing a plan? The Support policy explains how to reach the support team. For Enterprise pricing and contracts, contact sales .
Make the most of your included daily chat allowance and daily build credits . Chat usage follows its own pricing model, including a per-message price and a daily chat allowance. Free, Pro, and Business plans include the daily chat allowance for messages in Chats and Chat mode , including Lovable in Slack and Telegram . It resets every day at 00:00 UTC and does not roll over. See Chat costs . Free, Pro, and Business plans also include 5 daily build credits at no extra cost. They reset every day at 00:00 UTC and do not roll over. On Free plans, daily build credits are capped at 30 per calendar month . After you reach the monthly cap, daily build credits resume on the 1st of the next calendar month at 00:00 UTC . Pro and Business plans do not have a monthly cap for daily build credits.

Student and teacher discount
Students and teachers at universities, colleges, and schools get 50% off for up to 12 months on the Pro plan with 100 credits per month, billed monthly. Lovable verifies your status on lovable.dev/students by checking the domain of your account’s email address (the part after the @) against a list of academic institutions. Verification is available only to workspaces on the Free plan, and each person can use the discount once.
1
Sign in with your academic email
Create your Lovable account with your institution’s email address, or sign in to the account that already uses it. Lovable verifies the email address your account is registered with, and that address cannot be changed. If your existing account uses a personal email, create a new account with your academic email. See the tip below for moving your projects into the new account’s workspace.
2
Verify your status
Open lovable.dev/students and select Verify student status . Choose Student or Teacher , select Next , answer the short questions about your studies or teaching, confirm your status, and select Verify Status Now .
3
Subscribe to Pro for Students
After verification, the same dialog shows the Pro for Students plan at the discounted price. The plan name also covers verified teachers. Select Upgrade and complete checkout. To return to the plan later, select Get your discount on lovable.dev/students . Your verification stays valid for 12 months, so subscribe within that time. The discount then applies for up to 12 months from the start of your subscription, or until you cancel. Lovable does not offer the discount again after you cancel.
The discount has these limits:
It applies only to the Pro plan with 100 credits per month, billed monthly. Higher Pro credit tiers, annual billing, and other plans are not available with the discount.
Credit top-ups are charged at standard prices.
The discount is tied to the account that verified and cannot be transferred to another account or person.
My institution’s email domain is not recognized If the verification dialog shows Invalid email , either your address is a personal one, which Lovable cannot verify, or your institution’s domain is not on the list yet. For an institution domain, select Why did this happen? and then Request review . Lovable reviews requested domains and adds eligible institutions to the list. If Lovable adds yours, verify again from lovable.dev/students .
If your projects are in a workspace owned by an account with a personal email, verify with a new account that uses your academic email and subscribe there. Then invite your personal account to the new workspace, because you can move a project only between workspaces you belong to. Signed in as your personal account, select Move workspace in each project’s settings to move the project there. See Move a project to another workspace .

Manage your subscription
Go to Plans & credit usage ( Settings → Plans & credit usage ) to manage your subscription, billing information, payment method, and invoices.
From Plans & credit usage , you can:
Upgrade or downgrade your plan
Switch between monthly and annual billing
View upcoming invoices and payment history
Update billing and payment information
Select Manage on your plan card to open the Manage plan dialog.
The dialog shows your current plan and daily credits, with Upgrade plan , Downgrade to free , Edit billing information , and Invoices & payments , and a Gift card balance card where you can redeem a gift card. Coupon codes are applied at checkout when you upgrade.
To check which plan your workspace is on, or whether a new subscription has activated, you can also ask Lovable in the project chat . You can change your plan only in Plans & credit usage .
​
Upgrade your subscription tier
If you consistently run out of subscription credits before your renewal date, upgrading is usually more cost-effective than repeated top-ups.
For one-time top-ups, auto top-up, and how credits are spent, see Credits and usage .
To upgrade:
Go to Settings → Plans & credit usage .
Choose your desired credit amount from the credits per month dropdown.
Select Upgrade .
Complete the upgrade in the Stripe portal.
When you upgrade to a higher credit allocation, you do not receive a full new grant immediately. Your monthly allocation is updated, and you receive the difference. For example, upgrading from 100 credits to 200 credits adds 100 credits, not 200. Your existing unused credits keep their original expiry date.
​
Downgrade your subscription tier
Use these steps to move to a lower paid tier on the same plan. To move to the Free plan instead, see Cancel your subscription .
To downgrade to a lower paid tier:
Go to Settings → Plans & credit usage .
Choose your desired credit amount from the credits per month dropdown.
Select Downgrade .
Complete the downgrade in Lovable and then in the Stripe portal.
Your current plan remains active until the end of the current billing period.
Downgrading between paid tiers keeps your existing credits When you downgrade to a lower paid tier, including from Business to Pro, your unused monthly plan credits and rollover credits stay available until their original expiry date. Your new monthly credit allocation applies from your next billing period.
Downgrading to Free freezes subscription credits If you downgrade to the Free plan, unexpired credits granted through your paid subscription cannot be used while your workspace is on Free. This includes monthly plan credits and rollover credits. If you upgrade to a paid plan again before those credits expire, you can continue using them until their original expiry date. Top-up credits you purchased while subscribed and any bonus credits can still be used on Free until they expire.
​
What stops working when you downgrade
When your workspace moves to a lower plan, features that are not included in the new plan stop working. Examples include:
Scheduled security scans and scheduled data exports (Enterprise) stop running. Stopped scans no longer use credits.
Verified email sign-up (Business and Enterprise) stops automatically adding new sign-ups to your workspace. Existing members keep their access.
Announcement banners (Business and Enterprise) stop showing to workspace members.
Publishing to your workspace (Business and Enterprise): an app that is published to workspace members or to a custom audience stays live, but Lovable does not publish new changes to it until you change its website access to public or upgrade again. See What happens to my published app if I downgrade or cancel my plan? .
Hiding the Lovable badge (paid plans): if you hid the Edit with Lovable badge on a published project, your live site keeps it hidden until you publish again. On that next publish, Lovable warns you, then brings the badge back and disables the project’s Hide Lovable badge setting. To hide the badge again after upgrading, enable the setting again in Project settings → Publishing .
For most features, Lovable keeps your configuration, so they resume automatically if you upgrade again. Plan-dependent workspace defaults, such as default project visibility and publishing permissions, reset to the new plan’s defaults and are not restored by a later upgrade.

Switch to annual billing
You can switch from monthly to annual billing on Pro and Business plans from Settings → Plans & credit usage .
Annual billing offers a discounted monthly rate and higher rollover limits for unused credits. Monthly plan credits on annual billing are still issued monthly and expire 1 month after the annual billing period ends.
Go to Settings → Plans & credit usage .
Find the Pro or Business plan card.
Enable Annual . The plan card updates to show the discounted monthly rate for annual billing.
Select the monthly credit amount you want from the credits per month dropdown.
Select Switch to annual and then confirm the change in checkout.
When you switch to annual billing, you are charged for the annual plan at checkout. Monthly plan credits continue to be granted each month.
​
Manage billing, payment method, and invoices
Go to Settings → Plans & credit usage to view upcoming invoices, payment history, and billing information.
To update your payment method (for example, to change or add a credit card) or edit billing details, select Manage on your plan card. Lovable redirects you to the Stripe billing portal, where you can:
Add, replace, or remove a credit card or other payment method.
Change the default payment method used for renewals and top-ups.
Update your billing name, address, and tax details.
Download past invoices and receipts.
Payment methods and billing details are stored by Stripe, not in Lovable, so this portal is the only place to view or change them.
​
Cancel your subscription
Canceling your paid subscription moves your workspace to the Free plan. Lovable has no separate cancel action and no email address for cancellation requests. You cancel yourself from Plans & credit usage .
To cancel your subscription:
Go to Settings → Plans & credit usage .
Select Manage .
In the Manage plan dialog, select Downgrade to free .
Complete the downgrade flow.
Your current plan remains active until the end of the current billing period. After that, your workspace moves to Free.
The credits per month dropdown on the plan card only lists paid tiers, so it cannot be used to move to Free. Use Manage → Downgrade to free instead.
​
Cancel from your phone
The Lovable mobile app has no cancel option, so on a phone you cancel in the browser. Open lovable.dev in your phone’s browser, go to Settings → Plans & credit usage , and follow the same steps. This applies on both iOS and Android.
Subscribed through the iOS app? Plans purchased inside the iOS app go through the App Store and work differently: each purchase covers one month and does not renew automatically, so you have nothing to cancel. Your plan card in Settings → Plans & credit usage shows Managed by App Store in place of the Manage button, along with the date your plan runs until. When that date passes, your workspace moves to the Free plan on its own, and you are not charged again unless you make a new purchase. Because these plans do not renew, they also do not appear under Settings → [Your name] → Subscriptions on your iPhone or iPad. See Lovable mobile app for more on iOS and Android billing differences.

FAQ
When do plan changes take effect?
Upgrades take effect when the upgrade is completed. If you upgrade to a higher credit allocation, you receive the difference between your previous allocation and your new allocation. Downgrades take effect at the end of your current billing period. Your current plan remains active until then.
What happens to my credits if I cancel my subscription?
Your current plan remains active until the end of the current billing period. After that, your workspace moves to the Free plan and your unused monthly plan credits and rollover credits are frozen. You cannot use them on Free, and they are not refunded. If you upgrade again before they expire, you can use them until their original expiry date. For more about credit expiry and rollover behavior, see Credits and usage .
What happens to my unused credits if I switch between paid tiers?
Your unused monthly plan credits and rollover credits stay available until their original expiry date when you move between paid tiers, in either direction. Switching between Pro and Business, or to a different Pro or Business tier, only changes your monthly credit allocation: immediately when you upgrade, and from the next billing period when you downgrade. For a full overview of credit expiry and rollover, see Credits and usage .
Can I switch from monthly to annual billing?
Yes. You can switch from monthly to annual billing on Pro and Business plans from Settings → Plans & credit usage . Annual billing offers a discounted monthly rate and higher rollover limits for unused credits. Monthly plan credits on annual billing are still issued monthly and expire 1 month after the annual billing period ends.
How can I change my billing information?
Go to Settings → Plans & credit usage , select Manage on your plan card, then choose Edit billing information . Lovable redirects you to the Stripe billing portal, where you can update your billing name, address, and tax details.
Where do I find or update my credit card or payment method?
Payment methods are stored in the Stripe billing portal, not in Lovable. Go to Settings → Plans & credit usage , select Manage on your plan card, then choose Invoices & payments to open the Stripe billing portal. From there, you can add, replace, or remove a card and change the default payment method used for renewals and top-ups.
Who can I talk to about my plan or billing?
On paid plans, contact the support team through the support form . The support team handles billing issues, refund reviews, and plan questions. On the Free plan, the support form is not available. Use the docs assistant on any docs page or ask the community on Discord . For Enterprise pricing and contracts, contact sales . See the Support policy for all support options by plan.
How do I download my invoices?
Go to Settings → Plans & credit usage , select Manage on your plan card, then choose Invoices & payments . Lovable redirects you to the Stripe billing portal, where you can find past invoices and receipts.
Is there a discount for students and teachers?
Yes. Verified students and teachers get 50% off the Pro plan with 100 credits per month, billed monthly, for up to 12 months. Verify your academic email on lovable.dev/students while your workspace is on the Free plan, then subscribe to Pro for Students . See Student and teacher discount .

Troubleshooting
I cannot pay for my subscription. What should I do?
Payment setup may occasionally fail because of an issue with Stripe. Check your payment details and retry. If the problem persists, try a different payment method.
I paid but still do not have access to my plan. What should I do?
First check whether the subscription has come through. Your current plan and subscription status are shown in Settings → Plans & credit usage , and you can also ask Lovable in the project chat , for example Has my subscription activated? If your plan still has not changed, contact support at support@lovable.dev .
I upgraded but have not received credits. What should I do?
You receive additional credits only if you upgrade to a plan or tier with more included credits. If you upgraded and still have not received the additional credits, contact support at support@lovable.dev .
I downgraded to Free. Why can't I use some of my credits?
If you downgrade to the Free plan, unexpired credits granted through your paid subscription are frozen. This means you cannot use monthly plan credits or rollover credits while your workspace is on Free. If you upgrade to a paid plan again before those credits expire, you can continue using them until their original expiry date. Top-up credits you purchased while subscribed and any bonus credits can still be used on Free until they expire.
