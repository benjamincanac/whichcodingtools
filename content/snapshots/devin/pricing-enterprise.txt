Billing
Enterprise
Copy page Copy page
How Devin Enterprise contracts are billed, how admins track ACU consumption, and how to set organization and per-user ACU limits.
Copy page Copy page
Devin Enterprise customers are billed in Agent Compute Units (ACUs) at the rate set in their order form. Contact sales for pricing.
For how Devin meters work in general (sleep behavior, what counts toward consumption, tips for keeping costs down), see Usage .
​
Tracking ACU consumption
Enterprise customers can track ACU consumption at both the Enterprise and Organization level:
Enterprise admins view Enterprise ACU consumption at Settings > Consumption in Enterprise Settings.
Organization admins view Organization ACU consumption at Settings > Consumption Analytics within Organization Settings.
Any user can see the ACU cost of a specific session from Session Insights .
​
Setting Organization ACU limits
Enterprise admins can set per-Organization ACU limits from Settings > Organizations in Enterprise Settings.
Hover over the Organization name and click 'Manage'
Set Organization ACU limits and/or rename the Organization
Once set, an Organization can only consume up to its limit. All Devin activity stops once the limit is reached, and users see a message indicating the Organization has hit its ACU limit and to contact the Enterprise admin to raise it.
ACU limit reached warning message
​
Setting per-user ACU limits
Beyond per-Organization limits, Enterprise admins can cap each member’s monthly ACU consumption with usage tiers, IdP group mappings, and per-member overrides. See Usage policies .
​
Frequently asked questions
When will I be billed?
Enterprise customers are billed for ACUs as stated in their order form.
What are Enterprise ACUs?
Enterprise ACUs represent the work performed by Devin for customers on the Enterprise plan, which adheres more strictly to task planning and end-to-end testing. They are distinct from self-serve quota and on-demand credits, and are priced per the customer’s Enterprise order form.
How can I tell which Organization or user consumed the most ACUs?
Enterprise admins can break consumption down by Organization from the Consumption page in Enterprise Settings. Org admins can break it down by user from Consumption Analytics inside Organization Settings.
